# Machine-native systems

**How should heterogeneous intelligent machine components communicate, coordinate, authorize actions, represent uncertainty, and establish evidence without assuming conversational language is the universal internal substrate?**

This is a research program and canonical research record, not a product implementation. The initial baseline preserves the October 2–3, 2026 Work research session. Its literature cutoff is October 2, 2026 (America/New_York), not the end of October.

## Current position

> Consequential boundaries between intelligent components benefit from explicit semantic contracts, while internal intelligent computation may use whatever representation best preserves useful information.

This is a falsifiable working hypothesis. The accumulated evidence supports separation of intelligence, authority, and evidence more strongly than universal replacement of general-purpose models. It does not demonstrate industry-wide LLM overuse, one universal communication format, or end-to-end superiority of the proposed combination.

## Research discipline

**existing evidence → identify actual gap → targeted retrieval → experiment**

Search this repository before retrieving external material. Reuse prior citations and evidence. Preserve contradictions and source limitations. Cite exact revisions where possible. Do not rerun broad searches to regenerate another memo. Do not implement a product or experiments without an explicit research task.

The original reports remain substantially as produced. Organizational documents are derived syntheses, not additional research results. Repository examples come from SHAD0W, TEMPER, CipherLoop, TraceForge, AetherForge, and Fracture; they are artifacts from one author, not independent experimental replications.
