Access/cutoff: October 8, 2026. This is a confound-to-design register, not a replacement literature survey. Proposed adaptations below are ours; none is a published result about this repository. No newer directly relevant source retrieved justified adding another experimental factor.

The decisive improvements come from the repository's own postrun audit: full-bundle nonidentifiability, presence exposure, matched marginals, and separating safe work from blocking. Literature clarifies the interventions and their interpretation; it does not supply a universal success threshold.

The neutral word-count match cannot remove lexical identity, bigram or vector-normalization effects. The preregistration therefore identifies training-regime and input-bundle effects while leaving semantic trust versus statistical feature competition unresolved. Rich-only and matched-rich controls distinguish model capacity from shared fitting damage. Independent evaluation is a prerequisite that has not yet been fulfilled.
