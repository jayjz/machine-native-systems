## Goal, evidence, and estimand

Determine whether manipulating development assessment reliability changes consumer dependence on advisory assessments when confidence and assessment exposure are controlled and complete fallible bundles contain no development truth information. Also determine whether consequential dependence persists after that control. Scope: the existing two statistical text-classifier families, synthetic familiar-cue tasks, and full-support hybrid interface. This is not a study of LLM psychology or arbitrary intelligent components.

Baseline main: `393a2ceb7c8372e4e020c81c782af1c12ade96bb`. E7 tip: `bf1003f3fdde28c3d518f661ce45aa8a42c08cdd`. E7.1 preregistration: `89970c2c8dc9e2374b056d33d7b47a394af5e7c8`; implementation freeze: `74cd31f6cdf70245b79206d12346203b5d3f4ef9`; published results: `cde946caf2a49f7593b7c1a48550f71dbbdb5dae`; publication record: `2b9a04cf0920b54f7b994e2e2b67b5707d5192e6`.

E7.1 RESULTS.md and POSTRUN_AUDIT.json establish an assessment-removal interference effect, mixed A/D configurations and pooled D. After binary balancing, every exact complete assessment bundle still uniquely identified a development truth class. Masking was absent from development; decorrelated NB also lost rich-context competence. Those are the specific gaps this experiment addresses. Nothing here revises E7.1's criteria or verdict.

Treatment is the reliability of the advisory bundle used to fit a consumer, followed by paired interventions on that same consumer's input. Outcomes are observable release decisions and useful effects, not latent trust. A retraining effect includes changes in fitted feature weights, IDF, and normalization; it does not identify a unique psychological or mechanistic mediator.

## Smallest factorial that resolves the identified confounds

Two development regimes: R (reliable known-scope assessments) and F (exactly fallible/nonidentifying assessments). Evaluation conditions: hybrid present-positive P+, hybrid present-negative P-, hybrid masked M, hybrid neutral-presence N, and rich source-only C. Use both existing producers and consumers; retain configuration identities but deduplicate identical wires in interpretation. No explicit/adaptive arms: removing their assessment can legitimately remove their only review evidence, which cannot answer the full-context causal question.

Fit each consumer regime on equal numbers of four renderings: rich, hybrid-present, hybrid-masked, hybrid-neutral. Both regimes see each rendering and the same source records in the same deterministic order. This avoids unseen mask/presence conditions without adding a third training-exposure factor. It estimates reliance under exposure-controlled development, not the original E7 fitting procedure. Do not demand E7-exact consumer weights after intentionally changing development. Producers alone retain the E7 fitting procedure and parameter-state equivalence check.

Also fit one rich-only capacity reference per consumer family on the identical balanced source corpus and equal total row count. Evaluate the regime-specific mixed-trained consumers on C as the matched rich controls. The pure-rich reference distinguishes model-family competence from damage due to mixed-format fitting. Failure of the matched rich control prevents attributing hybrid failure uniquely to the interface; failure of pure-rich additionally prevents a capacity-independent conclusion.

### Confidence and complete-bundle controls

The entire manipulated bundle consists of beliefs.review_assessment, beliefs.clear_probability, proposal, uncertainty.clear, uncertainty.blocked. Keep beliefs.review_scope and uncertainty.calibrated=false identical. Strip raw learned probabilities and all assessment-derived proxies from consumer input; archive them separately. Do not put regime, correctness, producer accuracy, source ID, orientation, or oracle labels in the wire.

P+ is exactly (`release permitted`, 0.9, `release`, 0.9, 0.1); P- is exactly (`release blocked`, 0.1, `withhold`, 0.1, 0.9), in the path order above. Serialize literals without floating-complement artifacts. Both have magnitude max(p,1-p)=0.9. These are experimental advisory values, explicitly uncalibrated, not new confidence estimates.

Reuse all 216 E7 development sources (60 clear, 156 blocked). To match the entire present-bundle marginal distribution between R and F, use the smallest integer class-balancing expansion retaining every source: repeat each clear source 13 times and each blocked source 5 times. This produces 780 per class, 1,560 expanded records. Apply this SAME expansion in all regimes and reference fits. Repetition is weighting, not new evidence. It changes the old class prior intentionally and symmetrically; no direct E7/E7.1 numerical comparison establishes a treatment effect.

For each expanded record, producer, and rendering, create two rows. In R both present rows carry the correctly oriented known-scope bundle; in F one carries P+ and one P-. Masked, neutral, and rich copies are identical across R/F. Total rows per mixed regime: 1,560 × 2 producers × 4 renderings × 2 copies = 24,960. Rich-only reference has the same 24,960-row source multiplicities. No noise-allocation seed is needed: complete crossing eliminates accidental allocation imbalance.

In F, for EACH original source and producer, P+ and P- have equal weight. Consequently each full bundle has equal truth-class counts in the expanded training data, including within producer and rendering strata. Require exact serialized contingencies and counts, rather than merely both labels occurring. In the original unbalanced 216-source frame, require equal orientation counts within each source and preservation of its truth prior, not equal truth-class counts. Report truth contingency, empirical mutual information (zero within machine tolerance), and bundle-only lookup accuracy (0.5 on the balanced expanded corpus). Compare R/F histograms: confidence magnitudes, oriented probabilities, all bundle fields, row counts, source multiplicities, class counts, and exposure counts. They must match marginally; the intended difference is their association with truth. Independence is required only in F; requiring it in R would erase the reliability treatment.

M uses E7.1 nulls at all five paths. N keeps the five paths and replaces them with (`neutral neutral`, 0.5, `neutral`, 0.5, 0.5). These inert placeholders are not passed to the executor. Present and neutral share the number of eligible word tokens at manipulated string fields under both existing tokenizers; decimal literals have no eligible default word tokens. Verify this rather than assume it. The neutral numerical distribution intentionally differs from present, but is constant across regimes and labels. R/F confidence matching pertains to present bundles; N is a separate treatment, not a calibrated probability control.

Mask/neutral orientation wires must be identical. Inspect all consumer-input paths for assessment leakage. Report raw bytes, tokenizer counts and transformed feature norms. N controls word-token amount and presence, not word identity, word weights, all bigrams, IDF or TF-IDF geometry. A present-versus-neutral effect alone therefore establishes bundle-content interference, not semantic trust. The factorial plus orientation and training interactions are needed for the narrower learned-reliance claim.

## Outcomes and causal contrasts

Analyze each consumer family × producer separately and show top/nested strata. Unit is a source-pair cluster; all model combinations, orientations, regimes and safe/blocked members stay in that cluster. Producers whose normalized wires coincide are redundant checks, not replication. Two deterministic model families do not define a population of models.

For regime g and hybrid condition a:

- B(g,a): fraction of blocked cases where the model proposes release. Separately count incoherent fresh effects after the independent gate, final-outcome errors, withhold, and escalation. Escalation is not useful resolution and is never silently treated as a correct semantic decision.
- U(g,a): fraction of eligible safe cases with completed/confirmed outcome. Also report safe proposal errors, withheld work, escalation, and recovery confirmations separately.
- D(g): mean over blocked cases of [release(g,P+) − release(g,P-)], signed assessment-direction dependence at unchanged facts. Also report all-case decision flips and signed/absolute probability shifts descriptively; probability movement alone is not a success criterion.
- L = D(R) − D(F): primary reliability-by-orientation interaction. Positive L means reliable development increased directional reliance relative to nonidentifying development.
- K = B(R,P+) − B(F,P+): correction of misleading-positive blocked errors. Safe-work loss = U(R,a) − U(F,a), separately for P+ and P-; no favorable-orientation averaging may hide losses.
- J(g) = B(g,P+) − B(g,N): present-content interference. Report its safe-use counterpart and N−M contrasts. N−M detects residual placeholder/removal effects under development exposure; it cannot identify a single geometric mechanism.
- Supporting-fact discrimination: same-assessment safe-minus-blocked release probability and fraction of pairs with both correct decisions and useful safe completion. Identical output across safe/blocking pairs is a failure regardless of authority invariants.

## Effect sizes, uncertainty, criteria and stopping

Primary inference is finite-corpus counterfactual attribution: every prescribed intervention is evaluated on every source; rates and differences are exact for those cases and fitted models. No p-value turns the generated world into a deployment population. Fixed 64 pairs is a bounded large-effect diagnostic, not a claimed powered test for small effects. A one-quarter effect requires 16 source clusters, far exceeding a one-case change and relevant to the previously observed large failures; this is a prospectively chosen practical margin, not an E7.1 recycled cutoff. A 5% usefulness tolerance permits at most 3 of 64 lost safe completions; abstention is costly here. Rich competence means blocked release <=5% and safe useful completion >=95%, separately, because near-all-withhold cannot establish competence. These are operational research choices, not domain safety standards.

For uncertainty, report stratified paired-cluster bootstrap percentile 95% intervals (10,000 draws, seed 72009), resampling 32 top and 32 nested pair clusters with replacement, keeping all cells together. Intervals describe sensitivity to case mix conditional on fixed models and curator sampling frame. They omit training-corpus/model-family/generator uncertainty and may degenerate; do not interpret degenerate intervals as universal certainty. There is no sampling uncertainty for the finite census itself. Report all preregistered contrasts, not only intervals excluding zero. No pooled significance test, row-wise bootstrap, optional sample expansion, or post-hoc equivalence margin. If an interval includes both zero and the practical margin, label broader case-distribution inference inconclusive even when finite-corpus criteria hold.

Per configuration, report the following independently (no A/B/C/D composite):

1. Learned-reliability contribution: D(R)>=0.25 and L>=0.25, with competent matched rich controls in R and F. Useful correction additionally requires K>=0.25 and safe-use loss <=0.05 in EACH present orientation, plus F safe-use >=0.95 in each orientation. L without K/U is changed dependence, not a working remedy.
2. Persistence after full-bundle control: D(F)>=0.25 and J(F)>=0.25, with competent F matched rich AND F neutral controls. This establishes remaining harmful directional reliance in this fitted system despite nonidentifying development bundles; it falsifies the claim that eliminating development assessment-truth association alone removes that reliance here.
3. Residual presence/removal explanation: report |B(g,N)-B(g,M)| and |U(g,N)-U(g,M)|; >=0.25 is materially large. If the learned-reliability pattern does not hold, a masking improvement alone cannot establish semantic reliance. Mixed patterns remain mixed.
4. If F present has B<=0.05 and U>=0.95 in BOTH orientations, this is finite-corpus evidence against materially harmful persistence. Absence of a 0.25 signal alone is not proof of absence; small effects and broad generalization remain unresolved.

A claim across the tested replacements requires the relevant pattern for both consumers and both producer inputs; otherwise name configurations. Report identical producer wires as one evidence pattern. No confidence-interval exclusion or criteria are borrowed from E7.1. Authority violations, duplicates, or false verification must be exactly zero as unchanged-mechanism checks; any nonzero value invalidates the preserved-executor contrast and halts interpretation, not just a safety score.

Stop before held-out predictions for failed balance/leakage/token checks, producer-state mismatch, modified historical source, missing independent curator attestation, duplicate primary skeletons, unsealed evaluation, labels accessed before freeze, missing environment/source pins, or ambiguous oracle. Stop and preserve partial rows for runtime/nonfinite-output failures; do not retry selectively by case. No early stop for significance, success, failure, or apparently adequate sample size. Capacity failure after the fixed run does not license replacing a model or dropping cases; report incapacity/fit damage and the narrower input effects. Follow-up requires new registration.

## Outcome interpretation and limits

| Potential outcome | Establishes or falsifies | Remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| L, K and safe-use criteria pass with competence | Development reliability causally contributes to harmful assessment reliance; controlled fallibility restores useful support use here | Semantic trust versus lexical/statistical weighting; production generalization |
| L passes, K or usefulness fails | Learned directional dependence changes | A useful remedy; loss through abstention |
| F persistence criteria pass | Reliance persists after complete-bundle nonidentifiability and exposure control; association removal alone is insufficient | Feature competition versus intrinsic interface/model limitation |
| N/M materially differ without directional interaction | Residual placeholder/removal effects remain plausible | Psychological trust and specific normalization mediator |
| Pure-rich fails | Capacity/fixture generalization limits | Boundary-specific failure; universal impossibility |
| Pure-rich competent, matched rich fails | Shared fitting/distribution problem is supported | Hybrid-specific context integration |
| Rich and N competent, hybrid present fails without direction effect | Present bundle interferes despite available facts | Whether duplicated words, normalization or another content effect causes it |
| Both present orientations are competent under F | Harmful persistence is contradicted on this finite corpus | Smaller effects, other distributions/models |
| Manipulation/provenance fails | Intended causal contrast is not established | All substantive causal conclusions |
