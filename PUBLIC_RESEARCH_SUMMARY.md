# Machine-Native Systems

Machine-Native Systems asks how heterogeneous intelligent machine components should communicate, coordinate, authorize actions, represent uncertainty, and establish evidence without assuming conversational language is the universal internal substrate. The program studies what information must survive component boundaries and what independently establishes permission and outcome. It combines preserved literature synthesis with bounded comparisons, treating negative results as changes to the working model rather than reasons to preserve a preferred architecture.

## Current falsifiable working position

Explicit semantics around consequential state, authority, attempts, and evidence remain a hypothesis, with representational freedom inside components. E7/E7.1 materially complicate its semantic-sufficiency claim: a contract can preserve facts while a learned consumer fails to use them. The position weakens if information loss and integration costs outweigh useful coherence, recovery, or substitution benefits. Neither typed-format superiority nor universal explicit-boundary failure has been established.

## Durable questions

- What decision-relevant information must survive compression, novelty, and boundary evolution?
- Can intelligence change while transition ownership, authority, and accountability remain stable?
- What independently establishes an outcome, beyond a producer's explanation?
- How should uncertainty and dependent evidence change abstention, escalation, and action?
- Which coordination mechanisms remain coherent under faults and heterogeneous components, at acceptable full-system cost?

## Experiment progression

| Stage | Result and implication |
|---|---|
| [E4](experiments/e4-boundary/RESULTS.md) | Controlled prose, full JSON, and hybrid full-information representations tied in the implemented fixture world. Compact representation lost distinctions and produced disagreement/duplicate effects under optimistic defaults. No typed-format superiority. |
| [E4.1](experiments/e4-1/RESULTS.md) | Separating omission from defaults showed that fail-closed behavior prevented duplicates while sacrificing useful completion. Alternatives + attempt + status was the minimum sufficient subset among tested omissions, only for this consumer, fixtures, oracle, and receipt model. |
| [E7](experiments/e7/RESULTS.md) | Rich context outperformed explicit, hybrid, and inventory-first adaptive boundaries under learned novelty. Hybrid provided contradictory supporting facts that consumers often failed to use. Economical fixed/adaptive sufficiency weakened in this implementation. |
| [E7.1](experiments/e7-1/RESULTS.md) | Masking producer assessments improved novelty in several configurations with supporting facts unchanged, causally establishing assessment-bundle interference there. The preregistered pooled verdict remained **D / inconclusive**. Universal shortcut learning was not established; confidence fingerprints and other integration/fitting effects remain plausible. |

## Current frontier

Can a learned component distinguish when producer assessments should yield to contradictory supporting context, and can that failure be diagnosed cleanly before introducing context-recovery architecture? E7.2 is proposed, not executed. No context-recovery or escape-hatch architecture has been validated. The immediate frontier is diagnosis before redesign.

## Major limitations

The experiments use small same-author synthetic worlds: scripted E4/E4.1 consumers and two bounded statistical text-model families in E7/E7.1. Novel locations use familiar vocabulary; probabilities are uncalibrated; authority and receipt stores are trusted simulations. Crossed executions are correlated, not independent population samples. Valid permission, receipt integrity, and deterministic reproduction do not establish semantic correctness or deployment reliability. E7.1 masking also reduced some useful completion across the full set and is not a deployment-wide remedy. No general LLM, open-world, production-scale, or lifecycle-cost advantage is established.
