# Investigation record and hypothesis evolution

Derived provenance notes reconstructed from the available Work context. The imported reports and pasted transcript are preserved separately. This document does not pretend to be a complete verbatim execution log.

## Original scope

The bounded-decision investigation asked whether general-purpose conversational models are overused for recurring internal decisions and whether typed state, bounded policies, calibrated abstention, independent authority, execution, and evidence offer a better architecture. It explicitly requested supporting and contradicting evidence and no initial product proposal.

The communication investigation expanded the question to how heterogeneous intelligent components become coherent systems, examining fifteen mechanisms across distributed systems, formal methods, control, robotics, classical multi-agent systems, probabilistic inference, and contemporary LLM agents. It requested primary sources and an answer unconstrained by the chatbot metaphor.

The exact original prompts and the bounded-decision conversational synthesis are available in [the exported pasted transcript](work-session-pasted-transcript.txt). The completed communication investigation is available in [the full research map](../intelligent-component-communication-research-map.md).

## Trajectory of conclusions

1. Initial replacement premise remained plausible only for tasks with suitable state, actions, objectives, and success criteria.
2. Artifact review supported independence of authority more strongly than replacement of cognition. An LLM can operate beneath explicit enforcement; a deterministic system can violate the same principles.
3. Literature review distinguished transport, message semantics, coordination, inference, authority, and evidence. Syntactic interoperability does not guarantee semantic agreement.
4. Classical blackboards, probabilistic messaging, runtime assurance, capabilities, workflows, and formal protocols supplied strong prior art. The direction is largely composition of established traditions, not an entirely new architecture.
5. Recent latent communication and structured natural-language belief work prevented an exclusively symbolic conclusion.
6. Leading synthesis became semantic contracts at consequential boundaries, with multiple substrates and representational freedom internally. Its general end-to-end advantage remains unproven.

## Important negative findings

No industry prevalence estimate for unnecessary LLM reasoning; no universal ideal wire format; no general independent correctness oracle for informal goals; no proof that MAST failures are caused by prose; no universal exactly-once external execution; no unconditional asynchronous consensus progress; no general calibration protection against arbitrary shift; no completed controlled experiments in this research program.

## Evidence-strength distinctions retained

- Peer-reviewed formal results are conditional on assumptions and models.
- Benchmark results are scoped to tasks, baselines, models, and implementations.
- Official documentation describes interfaces and operational behavior, not evidence of architectural superiority.
- Position papers and security/semantic surveys supply arguments and taxonomies rather than deployment proofs.
- Repository source inspection supports narrow implementation observations; reported validation is not independent reproduction.
- Explanations may be useful without providing independently verifiable evidence.
