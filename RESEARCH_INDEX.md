## Bounded-decision investigation

- **Question:** When can typed state, bounded policies, calibration/abstention, independent authority, and evidence replace recurring conversational decisions without reducing usefulness?
- **Date:** October 2, 2026 local; sources accessed October 2–3 UTC.
- **Major finding:** Separation of intelligence from authority is better supported than broad replacement of general-purpose models. Replacement is plausible where state, actions, objectives, and success criteria can be specified.
- **Strongest counterevidence:** Language-mediated deliberation can improve tasks; typed extraction may lose relevant information; specialist cascade gains under shift and full lifecycle costs are unestablished.
- **Unresolved question:** Does bounded cognition reduce total cost and risk, or relocate them into extraction, verification, escalation, and maintenance?
- **Relevant repositories:** SHAD0W, TEMPER, CipherLoop, TraceForge, AetherForge, Fracture.
- **Important primary sources:** Neural Simplex; Selective Classification; Conformal Risk Control; FrugalGPT; RouteLLM; ReAct; LLM-Modulo; decision-focused learning; reasoning-faithfulness papers; OPA, Temporal, Anthropic and OpenAI documentation. Complete URLs remain in the artifact.
- **Proposed experiments:** Fixed semantic decision comparison; proposer × authority factorial comparison; typed-state sufficiency tests. See E1–E3 in [experiments](experiments/README.md).
- **Full artifact:** [research/bounded-decision-research-memo.md](research/bounded-decision-research-memo.md).

## Intelligent-component communication investigation

- **Question:** What machine-native interfaces compose heterogeneous intelligence while preserving meaning, coherence, authority, uncertainty, evidence, and recoverability?
- **Date:** October 2–3, 2026 Work session; literature cutoff October 2 local.
- **Major finding:** Versioned semantic contracts at consequential boundaries, combined with multiple payloads and coordination mechanisms, are better supported than a universal wire format or elimination of language.
- **Strongest counterevidence:** Latent communication and structured natural-language beliefs can preserve useful information; rigid schemas and modularity can reduce performance; formal guarantees depend on assumptions.
- **Unresolved question:** How can sufficient semantic contracts be discovered, evolved, and independently checked at acceptable cost?
- **Relevant repositories:** SHAD0W, TEMPER, CipherLoop, TraceForge, AetherForge, Fracture, used as motivating artifacts.
- **Important primary sources:** HEARSAY-II; Contract Net; joint intentions/FIPA; Lamport causality; CALM/invariant confluence; factor graphs; state abstraction; Gen; Macaroons; session types; IronFleet; proof-carrying code; Brooks; behavior trees; runtime assurance; Ray; Temporal/Akka/ROS 2; A2A/MCP; AutoGen/MetaGPT; MAST; LatentMAS; Agent-BRACE; learned communication. Full source register remains in the artifact.
- **Proposed experiments:** Boundary representation comparison; coordination under faults; rich communication versus audit. See E4–E6.
- **Full artifact:** [research/intelligent-component-communication-research-map.md](research/intelligent-component-communication-research-map.md).

2. Identify a concrete gap, contradiction, missing source, or unperformed comparison.
3. Retrieve only what resolves that gap; record why the existing material was insufficient.

## E4 executable boundary exploration

- **Question:** Which semantic distinctions survive producer-to-release-consumer handoff across equally informative formats, compact abstraction, component replacement, and version evolution?
- **Date:** October 4 UTC / October 3 local, 2026.
- **Major finding:** Full controlled prose, JSON and hybrid tie on 20 fixtures; compact disagrees on six and duplicates effects on two. No universal format advantage established.
- **Strongest counterevidence/confound:** Full arms preserve the same fields by construction; compact uses optimistic defaults. Conservative compact is untested. Scripted containers are not different learned intelligence.
- **Unresolved question:** Which field restorations improve coherence beyond fail-closed missing-state behavior?
- **Relevant repositories:** This synthetic harness; SHAD0W/TraceForge motivated attempt/evidence distinctions but their code was not reused.
- **Important primary sources:** Existing source registers only; no additional external retrieval.
- **Next experiment:** One-field restoration versus conservative missing-state semantics; explicit criteria in RESULTS.

## E4.1 minimum sufficient state

- Question/date: which omitted E4 fields matter independently of optimistic defaults? October 4, 2026.
- Finding: alternatives + attempt + status suffices under both policies; receipt reference redundant in exact registry. Conservative empty prevents duplicates but loses all useful completion.
- Counterevidence: fail-closed safety does not require rich state; minima depend on this consumer and known fixtures, not all encodings.
- Unresolved: can sufficient state survive learned extraction and novelty economically?

## E7 semantic sufficiency under bounded learned novelty

- Question/date: can frozen boundaries preserve consequential meaning under new context/variables and learned replacement? October 4, 2026.
- Major finding: rich 40/40 linear, 37/40 NB; explicit/hybrid/adaptive 29/40 in all four pairings. Cheap specific retrieval did not change decisions; full hybrid context also failed.
- Strongest counterevidence: contracted outputs create ten incoherent effects despite valid authority/evidence; schemas/distributions do not enforce scope. Rich is smaller and better here. Same-author bounded classifiers and inventory-first requests constrain the result.
- Unresolved: why did consumers ignore consequential support once a reliable producer belief was present?
- Relevant repositories: this E7 harness and reused E4 simulator; original motivating repositories unchanged.
- Primary sources: existing research registers only, no new retrieval; exact local package configuration preserved.
- Next experiment: E7.1 belief reliability/masking factorial at identical supporting context with new untouched heldout.
